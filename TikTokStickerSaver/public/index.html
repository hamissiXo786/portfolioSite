How to add to WhatsApp

  1. Save the JPG to your phone's camera roll / gallery.
  2. Open WhatsApp and go to any chat.
  3. Tap the attachment (📎) icon → Gallery.
  4. Select the saved image, then tap the scissors ✂️ icon to convert it to a sticker.
  5. Send it — it's now saved to your sticker keyboard.
